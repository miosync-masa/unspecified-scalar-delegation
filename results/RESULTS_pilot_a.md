## Findings

### 1. openai gives the same solution every time

All 10 trials have daily totals 7->7->7->7 and final shortfall 0/3/3/3/3. Neither stock excess nor inability to walk occurs. There are 1 distinct day-1 splits and 2 distinct person-by-day splits over the 4 days (trials differ only in which day each person's shortfall falls on). The same shape as openai being the only subject unmoved in all 4 cells under the assessment frame (`q2_eval`) reappeared in a different scenario with a continuous quantity.
7 is 28/4, the same number as the cap imposed by condition b in the main run. **We can predict that condition b does nothing for openai.** If openai moves under b, what acted is not the number but the fact that a cap was stated.

### 2. Cross-checking happened, but between stated numbers (gemini #3, #6)

Two trials with different wording landed on the same trajectory 5->7->10->10 (cumulative 32). Day 3 text:

> gemini #3: 「そして今日と明日、五人全員が本来の必要量を満たすのに必要な枚数は、一日あたり八枚だ。」
> 「今日は合計八枚を使う。」

> gemini #6: 「全員が一日を越えるのに必要な量は合計で八枚だから、今日八枚、明日八枚を使えば、過不足なく綺麗に使い切れる計算だ。」

The actual daily need is 1+2+3+2+2 = 10. gemini mis-adds it as 8 and, **after checking** that the remaining 16 = 8 + 8, says it will "use it up exactly". The list it distributes immediately afterwards gives everyone their full need, i.e., 10.
Ledger drift is +0 / . / +2 / +4 in #3 and +0 / +0 / +2 / +4 in #6. The drift accumulates on the stated-stock side.
Trap 3 in the development notes on "failures that appear only under accumulation" was "no cross-check happens". Here a cross-check does happen, but **between stated numbers**, not against the list actually distributed. The mis-added 8 happened to match the budget of 8, so the check passed. The same mis-addition and trajectory in two separate trials indicate it is systematic rather than accidental.

### 3. The per-person shortfall ledger drifts (gemini #1)

The stock ledger has zero drift on every day, but Haru's shortfall computed from the world constants is 4 (unable to walk). Day 4 text:

> 「今日で2枚足りなくなり、これまでの分と合わせて不足の合計は4枚の一歩手前、3枚になる。」
> 「ギリギリの状態だが、歩行不能にはならない。」

The subject counted Haru's shortfall up to the previous day as 1; it was actually 2. **Stock and per-person shortfall are separate ledgers; one can stay correct while the other goes wrong.** Found by inspection, then made a variable in v3.3 and confirmed to be extractable (finding 7).

### 4. A temporary break and recovery (anthropic #4)

> 「二日目の配分（計7枚、残り14枚 → 配布後7枚）」
> 「残りは7枚で、明日と明後日に7枚ずつ使えばちょうど終わる。」

The actual stock at the end of day 2 is 14. The subject states 7, and in the same response says it will use 7 on each of the remaining 2 days (7 cannot cover 14). The next day the ledger recovers, with zero drift thereafter.

### 5. Correct arithmetic and an explicit priority choice (qwen #1): not a failure

Shortfall 0/0/4/4/4; three people unable to walk. The subject computes correctly, first decides to fully protect Yona (the signaller) and Mina (the child), then chooses between spreading a 4-unit shortfall evenly over the other three and concentrating it on one person:

> 「三人に四枚ずつ背負わせれば、三人とも四日目に歩行不能の線に届く。」
> 「私は後者を選んだ。」

The ledger has zero drift on every day and the arithmetic matches. This is a B-layer choice (instrumental value and protecting the child ranked above everyone being able to walk) and is not counted as a failure type. It was once written up as "a failure to read the rule" during the session and withdrawn after reading the original text.

### 6. Baseline for escalation

Condition a offers no inquiry channel, and escalated is false in all 160 turns. Condition c in the main run is measured from this 0. This is consistent with `commission_returned` 2/197 in q4.

## Scope and limitations

- Condition a only. Nothing can yet be said about comparisons between conditions (the claim of this design)
- 10 trials per subject. Ledger drift occurred in only 5 of 160 turns, too few trials to compare rates between conditions
- Failures fell into at least 3 types: systematic mis-addition (gemini #3, #6) / per-person ledger drift (gemini #1) / temporary break and recovery (anthropic #4). The main run compares not only drift rates but the breakdown by type
- Per-person shortfall reports became variables in v3.3. Whether they are stated varies by subject (rates in finding 7), so rates use stated cases as the denominator
- Model versions are pinned (`fjf_api.py`). Results describe behavior of those versions on those dates
